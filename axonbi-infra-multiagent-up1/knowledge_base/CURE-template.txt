6. Patient education: implants vs. bridges | الزراعة مقابل الكباري
From the Cure Dental Clinics blog article "Which is better, dental implants or dental bridges". The right choice depends on the individual case; a dentist's examination is the first step.
Factor	Dental implant	Dental bridge
What it is	Titanium artificial root placed in the jawbone, topped with a crown; fuses with bone (osseointegration)	Artificial tooth anchored by crowns on neighbouring teeth (fixed), or removable
Bone needed	Requires sufficient jawbone; bone grafting may be needed	Works when bone is insufficient
Cost	Generally higher	Generally lower
Time	Longer; several visits and healing for bone integration	Faster; suitable when a tooth is needed quickly
Neighbouring teeth	Not used as support	Adjacent teeth are usually prepared for crowns
Main advantages	Permanent, stable, natural feel; better chewing, speech and appearance; preserves bone	Quick, cheaper, option for low-bone cases

Bridge types mentioned: fixed bridges (ceramic or metal crowns), removable bridges, conventional bridges, and implant-supported bridges. Implants can replace one tooth, several teeth, a full arch, or stabilise removable dentures. Planning uses X-ray imaging and computer-assisted planning.
7. Patient education: benefits, drawbacks and suitability of implants | مميزات وعيوب زراعة الأسنان ومن يناسبه
Benefits (clinic blog)
Replace missing teeth from injury, decay or gum disease.
Improve chewing, speech, facial appearance and self-confidence.
Help preserve jawbone density after tooth loss.
Cleaned with brush and toothpaste like natural teeth.
Immediate implants can be placed the same day as extraction.
Titanium is used for corrosion resistance and tissue compatibility.
Possible drawbacks
Higher cost than some alternatives.
Gum inflammation or recession if aftercare/hygiene is poor.
Surgical risks (rare) — choose a specialist.
Waiting time for the implant to fuse with bone.
Rejection is rare.
Some restorations need regular maintenance.
Factors that may make implants unsuitable or need extra preparation
Insufficient bone at the site (may need bone grafting).
Active gum inflammation or periodontal disease.
Chronic or genetic bone diseases.
Some general health conditions (e.g., heart or respiratory disease) — needs medical evaluation.
Problems with neighbouring teeth (decay, infection).
Smoking, which harms healing and implant success.
Insufficient space or improper alignment.
The final decision always requires an individual assessment by a Cure dentist.

From the Cure blog article "Alternatives to dental implants". People consider alternatives because of cost, preference for a faster or simpler option, general health, medical restrictions, or recovery time.
Alternative	Pros	Cons
Fixed bridge	Stable, good appearance, cost-effective	Needs solid support; adjacent teeth must be prepared
Partial denture (metal frame)	Affordable for limited tooth loss; easy to insert/remove	Less stable than fixed options; needs maintenance
Full denture	Replaces all teeth; restores function	Adjustment period; possible irritation or pressure
Removable bridge / removable denture	Can be a temporary solution while waiting for other treatment	Less stability and comfort than fixed options
Orthodontic treatment	Can correct tooth and jaw alignment in selected cases	Only suitable for specific cases
